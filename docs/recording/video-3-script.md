# Video 3 — Committee presentation

Preparation script, NOT a transcript. Record the screen with webcam on and clear audio throughout. Maximum 5:00; aim for 4:00–4:40. Begin with the actual running app visible, not a title slide or screenshot. Use default settings initially: 5% target, 25 bp costs, 20% BIL floor, 60 months, VT override off. Keep the app running while speaking both opening statements.

[0:00 — LIVE app on screen]

Codex helped me implement the app, check calculations, and draft documentation.
The analysis and decision are mine; this is education, not investment advice.

I ask the committee to authorize a simulation-only pilot centered on 85% BIL and 15% VT. Allocate zero live capital to this process today. Explore ten to twenty percent VT and eighty to ninety percent BIL as a research range, not a guarantee that every allocation passes.

[0:35 — mandate and baseline table]

The client is a university club with a five-year horizon. Its requirements are a five-percent nominal annual expected-return target, at least twenty percent BIL, a ten-percent peak-to-trough investment loss limit, and full protection of the next twelve months of spending. There is one school-year withdrawal up to five percent of the reserve.

The benchmark is sixty percent VT and forty percent LQD. The two transparent baselines are equal weight and inverse volatility. With the full historical window and twenty-five basis points of implementation drag, equal weight misses the target and observed drawdown screen. Inverse volatility misses the target. The benchmark fails the BIL floor and drawdown screen.

[1:25 — show student pilot and constrained allocation]

The constrained grid portfolio is eighty percent BIL and twenty percent VT, at about five percent net estimated return and 3.09% annualized volatility. My fixed 85/15 candidate estimates 4.59% net and fails the current five-percent hurdle. That is why this is research rather than live approval.

[1:55 — turn ON last 36 months]

Changing only the estimation window moves the constrained allocation to ninety percent BIL and ten percent VT, with about 5.70% net estimated return. The fixed 85/15 candidate now estimates about 6.50% and passes the numerical screen. The decision panel updates, but the full live mandate remains failed. Sensitivity to historical estimates is a warning, not evidence that the shorter window is better.

[2:35 — Reset to base; enable VT override 6.5% and BIL override 3.0%]

Here is my forward stress, separate from historical means. I chose BIL 2.75 to 3.25%, informed by dated policy projections but allowing reinvestment risk. At BIL 3% and VT 6.5%, fixed 85/15 earns 3.275% net. The search finds no feasible five-point-grid allocation under these screens. I will not lower the target or hide the fixed-candidate failure behind a different optimization.

[3:00 — show point-in-time signal expander]

TB3MS is rate context for BIL, not an ETF return or automatic trading signal. The raw September thirtieth vintage knew August's 3.72%, not September's later 3.94%. Using observation-month labels alone would admit information unavailable at the decision date.

[3:25 — show decision and monitoring]

A passing historical screen cannot guarantee the hard loss cap. We have not implemented a protected dollar spending account. Monitor reserve and return failures, costs, timing and spending coverage. An eight-percent drawdown warning is proposed. At ten percent, stop further risk deployment and seek review; a trigger cannot guarantee execution before a breach.

Reconsider adoption only after the spending obligation and loss-limit feasibility are demonstrated, the return case survives the stated stresses, and the remaining limitations are resolved. Until then, simulation-only pilot and zero live allocation is the defensible committee decision.
