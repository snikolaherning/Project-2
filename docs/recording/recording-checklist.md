2. Publish latest app/evidence/docs, then launch the matching copy. Close unrelated tabs and notifications. Every video must be a screen recording with webcam on and clear audio, per professor’s email. Use readable zoom and a microphone test.
3. Record Video 1 ≤3:00, Video 2 ≤5:00, Video 3 ≤5:00. Aim below limits to allow pauses. Video 3 opens with the live app already visible and both required statements spoken over it.
4. Keep the videos distinct: evolution and a checked AI output; code/data flow; committee ask and live control demo. Do not spend all three retelling setup.
5. Upload to YouTube with the required audience access; obtain final URLs. Enable captions/transcripts and wait for processing if necessary. Check tickers, percentages, basis points, names and source attribution.
6. Save actual spoken transcripts as docs/transcript-video-1.txt, docs/transcript-video-2.txt and docs/transcript-video-3.txt. Preserve meaningful deviations from scripts. Add real timestamp indices, not estimated script cues.
7. Provide URLs, exact durations and transcripts to Codex for cross-checking. Do not label an unrecorded script as a transcript. Verify every unlisted video logged out; private repo access must separately be granted to instructional staff.
8. Complete submission-manifest.md with final links and frozen GitHub commit paths. Attach final decision-memo.md and validation-and-ai-use.md unchanged to Brightspace.
