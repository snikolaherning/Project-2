# Committee authorization and pilot gates

Proposed protocol for committee consideration, not a claim that a four-week pilot was completed. Student-selected action remains simulation-only pilot, 0% live allocation. Intended user: university club investment committee, classroom case. The research envelope is 10–20% VT / 80–90% BIL centered on 85/15; it is not an executable trade instruction or numerical-pass guarantee.

## What the committee can authorize today

Four weekly simulation reviews, with the analyst retaining a dated input/output snapshot at each review. No account connection, live order or live allocation is authorized. Freeze the original Lab 20 experiment and existing evidence; version later changes separately. The four-review cadence is an analyst proposal for a finite research pilot, not a student-reported past activity.

Each review records the fixed 85/15 results under the student BIL 2.75–3.25% and VT 6.5–7.5% scenarios, both historical covariance windows, cost assumptions and the current constraints. Display the fixed allocation and reoptimized search separately. Do not interpret failure of a finite grid as continuous infeasibility. Retain baseline verdicts and do not average away counterevidence.

## Stop or refuse the research output

| Trigger | Required response | Evidence retained |
|---|---|---|
| Missing/altered frozen CSV or raw vintage | Refuse calculation, restore verified input or create a deliberately versioned new snapshot | Hash, source URL/as-of date, failure message |
| Failed return/reserve/drawdown screen | Reject that candidate under those inputs; do not deploy capital | Inputs, fixed weights, rule results |
| No feasible grid mix | STOP scenario and report infeasibility, not silent target relaxation | Search rules and output |
| Spending account shortfall or absent protection | Reject live approval | Fixed dollar budget and actual protected cash-flow evidence |
| 8% investment drawdown warning (proposed) | Escalate for review | Cash-flow-adjusted NAV and high-water mark |
| 10% investment drawdown | Stop further risk deployment and seek committee decision; no guarantee that execution prevented breach | Market/transaction record and diagnosis |

## Evidence required before reconsidering live adoption

1. A dated, approved real dollar spending budget and evidence of due-date cash availability with principal protection; an ETF weight alone does not suffice. No real reserve amount or account evidence has been supplied in this classroom case.
2. A demonstrated protection design consistent with the hard 10% peak-to-trough limit, or explicit committee-approved mandate redesign. A historical drawdown screen or stop trigger cannot establish the hard guarantee. A redesigned universe/strategy requires a new analysis, not a label change on this app.
3. A defensible forward-return case that passes the stated stresses net of justified costs. Current student scenarios fail; more optimistic assumptions are not evidence by themselves.
4. Verified data-use rights for the intended deployment, reproducible outputs and independent launch evidence for the released version.

At the fourth review, the committee either continues explicitly scoped research, redesigns the mandate/process, or rejects the process. Completing four reviews does not automatically authorize live use. This makes the current negative evidence an actionable committee decision rather than an unfinished promise.
