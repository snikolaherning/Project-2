# COST — Buffett-inspired educational simulation

**Verdict: own the business**, subject to further work on its competitive advantage and management. This is a business-quality judgment, not a recommendation about the stock price.

I like earning power that shows up in cash. From FY2022 to FY2026, net income rose from 5,844.0 to 9,226.0, while the owner-earnings proxy rose from 3,501.0 to 9,382.0, all in USD millions. Every annual ROE check passes, and owner earnings remain positive throughout the period. That is an encouraging record, though the dip in owner earnings in FY2024 reminds me that cash generation does not climb in a straight line.

I would not mistake a tidy table for a complete understanding of a business. These facts alone do not establish a durable moat or management quality. Owner earnings here subtract total capital spending from operating cash flow; they do not distinguish maintenance from expansion spending. I would investigate those questions before committing capital. No price or margin-of-safety judgment is possible from this exercise.
